# Playbook: Defender Device Isolation

**Classification:** [TEMPLATE]

1. Confirm device identity in Microsoft 365 Defender portal.
2. Review active alerts and device timeline.
3. Obtain approval for isolation (business impact).
4. Isolate device (full or selective per policy).
5. Collect investigation package.
6. Proceed with malware/compromised-host IR playbooks.
7. Release isolation after remediation validation.
